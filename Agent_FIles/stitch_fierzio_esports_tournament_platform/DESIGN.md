## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the predatory kinetic intensity and hyper-tuned mechanical precision of elite competitive esports. The identity departs entirely from standard cold tech blues and synthetic purples, adopting an aggressive, carbon-and-crimson visceral presence. It balances high-velocity adrenaline with surgical telemetry discipline—built for elite players, franchise leagues, analysts, and demanding spectators.

The design philosophy combines **High-Contrast Cyber-Brutalism** with **Tactile Emissive Glass**:
- Deep void-carbon and dark obsidian surfaces that eliminate visual noise and eye fatigue during prolonged competition.
- Piercing crimson neon conduits, electric ruby flashes, and molten amber micro-accents directing visual hierarchy straight to real-time events, match state shifts, and lethal moments.
- Angular chamfers, telemetry-grade metadata readouts, and sub-pixel optical glow loops that give every bracket node, killfeed entry, and stat delta tactile feedback.

## Colors

The color architecture is built specifically for high-contrast dark arenas and competition broadcast displays. Light does not merely reflect; it emits from active components against light-absorbent, obsidian foundations.

### Surface System
- **Void Base (`#080a0f`):** The foundational absolute ground plane, optimizing OLED contrast ratios and framing live streams with zero glare.
- **Carbon Surface (`#0f1219`):** Primary paneling, telemetry side-rails, inactive brackets, and dock containers.
- **Carbon Elevated (`#171b26`):** Floating tournament cards, player stat dossiers, modal bodies, and team rosters.
- **Carbon Overlay (`#222736`):** Active dropdown drawers, context popovers, hovering rows, and tooltip surfaces.

### Interactive & Telemetry Accents
- **Electric Ruby & Crimson Neon (`#ff1a40` / `#ff2a55`):** Primary kinetic driver. Flags instant actions, live broadcast indicators, top-seeded contenders, round-winning clutch stats, and primary CTA triggers.
- **Crimson Core (`#e11d48`):** Structural borders, secondary button outlines, progress bar tracks, and defensive zones.
- **Molten Amber (`#f59e0b`):** High-priority alerts, objective captures, prize pool tallies, sudden-death states, and VIP tier achievements.
- **Text & Outlines:**
  - Full White (`#f8fafc`): 100% legibility for match scores, player tags, and vital metrics.
  - Subdued Slate (`#94a3b8`): Analytical labels, sub-headers, and static match data.
  - Ghost Carbon (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Passive structural dividers and perimeter boundaries.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses a tripartite division engineered for rapid optical parsing during peak combat action:

1. **Space Grotesk:** Drives high-tension display headers, tournament series titles, and team identifiers. Its geometric brutality and tight tracking project raw forward momentum.
2. **Geist:** Serves as the functional, low-distortion UI workhorse for platform rules, documentation, match descriptions, and chat logs.
3. **JetBrains Mono:** Enforces tabular alignment across variable live clocks, millisecond ping metrics, K/D spreads, bounty tallies, and tournament stage badges.

### Implementation Rules
- Tabular figures (`tnum`) are mandatory on all numeric readouts, match counters, and leaderboard placements.
- Tournament status badges, match phases (`GROUP STAGE`, `GRAND FINALS`, `MATCH POINT`), and telemetry stats must be rendered in uppercase using `label-sm` or `label-md` with widened letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an ultra-dense, low-latency modular workspace that treats the screen as an esports command console. Layouts maximize real-time telemetry visibility while ensuring stream embeds and dynamic brackets remain front and center.

### Breakpoints & Grid Model
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid system using `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Multi-bracket double-elimination trees can break into an edge-to-edge pan-and-zoom viewport with fixed HUD navigation rails.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Auxiliary tournament stats and chat panels collapse into slide-over carbon telemetry drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` canvas margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Tournament brackets convert into vertical linear matchup progressions or horizontally snapped single-match cards.

### Spacing Principles
- Information density is maintained using `space-xs` and `space-sm` for leaderboard rows, roster tables, and bracket metadata.
- Structural isolation is achieved using `space-lg` and `space-xl` gaps to segment major competition zones (e.g., Live Stream HUD vs. Interactive Bracket Tree vs. Player Loadout Inspector).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through carbon surface stacking combined with targeted crimson neon emission, replacing traditional blurred neutral drop shadows with photon-charged energy halos.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Void Ground):** `#080a0f`. Zero depth, purely light-absorbing matte finish. Used for the backdrop of the entire application.
2. **Level 1 (Carbon Substrate):** `#0f1219` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`. Used for persistent side menus, static table containers, and inactive bracket columns.
3. **Level 2 (Tactile Tournament Cards):** Semi-transparent `#171b26` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Deep ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
4. **Level 3 (Emissive / Active Match Node):** Background `#171b26` encased with a bright crimson perimeter: `1px solid rgba(255, 42, 85, 0.6)`. Outward photonic glow: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(255, 26, 64, 0.35)`.
5. **Level 4 (Floating Modals & Overlays):** `#222736` at 95% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, reinforced by `1px solid rgba(255, 42, 85, 0.4)` and a heavy ambient shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a sharp, mechanical corner radius scale (`roundedness: 1`), keeping corner curves extremely compact to convey military-spec esports hardware and industrial telemetry.

- **Micro Components (`0.125rem` / `2px` to `0.25rem` / `4px`):** Tournament badges, status chips, form inputs, button boundaries, and stats tags use an ultra-sharp `rounded-sm` profile.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Tournament bracket cards, modal dialogs, and video stream viewports.
- **Telemetry Chamfers:** High-status components (such as Match MVP badges, tier promotions, and kill streak trackers) utilize a 45-degree angled corner crop (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) with a 6px notch on opposite diagonal corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber (Deploy / Matchmake):** Solid Electric Ruby (`#ff1a40`) background with solid `#080a0f` bold typography. Hover triggers a pulsing crimson glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 42, 85, 0.55)`) and slight brightness overdrive. Active state shifts to `#e11d48`.
- **Secondary Laser (Spectate / Bracket View):** Translucent carbon fill (`rgba(255, 26, 64, 0.08)`) with a crisp 1px crimson border (`#ff2a55`) and white text. Hover deepens background opacity to `0.18`.
- **Tertiary Void:** `#0f1219` background, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border, transitioning to full white text on hover with zero glow.

### Cards & Tournament Bracket Nodes
- Constructed with `#171b26` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and a subtle 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
- When a matchup is **LIVE**, the card activates a dual-phase animated perimeter pulse shifting between `rgba(255, 42, 85, 0.25)` and `rgba(255, 42, 85, 0.75)`.
- Winning seed slots incorporate a 3px vertical accent bar on the left edge set in `#ff2a55`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Set with `rounded-sm` corners, `label-sm` monospaced type, and uppercase tracking.
- **LIVE Status:** Crimson fill (`rgba(255, 26, 64, 0.15)`) with an electric ruby label (`#ff2a55`) and an emissive 6px pulsing dot.
- **Tournament Stage / Seed:** Carbon gray fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Match Point / Sudden Death:** Molten amber fill (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`) with amber text (`#f59e0b`) and a sharp 1px border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Void-inset background (`#080a0f`), 1px ghost border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), and white text. Focus snaps the border instantly to `#ff2a55` with an inner radiant glow (`0 0 12px rgba(255, 42, 85, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 2px corner radius for checkboxes, sharp circles for radios. Checked state fills with `#ff1a40` and displays a pitch-black check icon.

### Stats Tables & Leaderboards
- High-density rows with alternating subtle `#0f1219` and `#131722` striping.
- Hovering a player row casts a full-width bottom highlight border in `#ff2a55`.
- Data deltas: Positive ratings in `#ff2a55`, warning states in `#f59e0b`, and passive stats in `#94a3b8`.

### HUD Overlay & Connectors
- **HUD Frame:** Minimalist 1px borders with technical corner crosses and integrated ping/FPS telemetry.
- **Bracket Connector Pipelines:** 1.5px orthogonal vector tracks (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`). Advancing match outcomes turn the active line into an illuminated `#ff1a40` conduit.
